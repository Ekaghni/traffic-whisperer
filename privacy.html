Privacy policy

Effective 18 November 2026

The short version. Traffic Whisperer has no accounts and does not send your game data to us. Your progress is saved on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and its ad partners, which use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play. You can turn off personalised ads in the game under Settings, then Privacy & ads.

Who we are

Traffic Whisperer ("the game") is made by Ekaghni Mukherjee, an independent developer based in India ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the game's data practices described here.

Contact and grievance officer: Ekaghni Mukherjee, ekaghni.mukherjee@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your phone: levels, stars, coins, gems, upgrades, settings, which purchases you own, and purchase transaction IDs (used only so a purchase is never granted twice). This data never leaves your device through us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. You can also erase your progress in Settings, using Reset progress.

What we collect

We do not run servers for the game and we do not collect, sell or share your personal data ourselves. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location.

Advertising

The game is free because it shows ads. We use Google AdMob, which runs an auction among the ad networks listed below to fill each ad. When an ad is requested or shown, these companies may collect or receive:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID), or an app-set ID if the advertising ID is unavailable or deleted
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it (country or city level)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, screen size, network type and app version
  • How you interact with ads, such as views, clicks and whether a video was completed
  • Diagnostic data about ad performance and crashes inside their software

They use this data to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, show ads based on your interests. Each company processes the data under its own privacy policy:

Our ad rules

Ads with a reward only play when you choose them, at most 12 a day. There are no banner ads and no ads when you open the game. Breaks between levels never appear before level 9 or right after a loss, and any purchase removes them.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google Play tells the game which product you bought and gives it an order or transaction ID, which the game keeps on your device to deliver the item once. The purchase code in the game is the Unity In-App Purchasing library from Unity Technologies (unity.com/legal/privacy-policy). Google's handling of payments is described in the Google Payments privacy notice.

Your choices

  • Personalised ads: in the game, open Settings, then Privacy & ads, and switch off Personalised ads. You will still see ads, but they will not be chosen using your advertising ID or activity.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states): where the law requires it, the game asks for your choices the first time you play, using Google's consent tool. You can change them any time in Settings, then Privacy & ads, then Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings, then Privacy or Google, then Ads).
  • Your data held by ad partners: because we do not hold your advertising data, requests to access or delete it go to each partner through the links above. We are happy to help: email us.

Your rights

India

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask for information about your personal data, ask for it to be corrected or erased, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to act for you. Write to our grievance officer at the email above. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

You can access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, withdraw consent at any time, and complain to your local data protection authority. Ads data is processed on the basis of your consent, collected through the consent form in the game.

United States

Residents of California and other states with privacy laws can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data for targeted advertising, using Privacy choices in the game (where shown) or by switching off Personalised ads. We do not sell personal data ourselves.

Children

Traffic Whisperer is meant for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and data about them has been collected, contact us and we will help get it deleted.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you reset progress or uninstall the game. Ad partners keep data for the periods described in their own policies. Data sent by the ad SDKs travels over encrypted connections.

International transfers

Our ad partners may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, Singapore, Ireland and China, under safeguards described in their policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
