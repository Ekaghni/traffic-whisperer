Draw the roads. Keep the city moving.

A low-poly traffic puzzle. Sketch roads with your finger, time the lights, and get every car home before the city gridlocks.

Coming soon onGoogle Play

Every level is one rush hour

Levels take two to five minutes. You build while traffic is paused, then press GO and watch your network cope.

  1. 1

    Plan

    Gates and districts are colour-coded. Drag to draw roads within your budget. Junctions form where roads meet, roads tunnel through hills and bridges fly over rivers.

  2. 2

    Run

    Cars pick their own routes and re-route around jams. Keep building while they drive: switch lights, add a roundabout, widen a road, tow a broken-down car.

  3. 3

    Win

    Deliver the goal number of cars before the jam meter fills. Earn up to three stars by keeping queues short and spending less.

Thirty cities, three worlds

Maple Suburbs teaches the basics. Riverside adds bridges and potholes. Downtown Metro brings sirens, rain and night shifts. Daily challenges and an endless mode come after that.

The city pushes back

New trouble arrives level by level, so there is always something to fix.

Potholes

Busy roads crack. Tap to repair before cars slow down.

Roadworks

Closures come with a countdown. Re-route or rush the crew.

Ambulances

Sirens need a clear path. Lights give way when they can.

Rush hour

Waves of cars test every junction you built.

Rain and night

Slick roads, longer braking, headlights after dark.

Free to play, fair about it

The game is free and paid for by ads and optional purchases. These rules are built into the game.

  • Ads that give you something, like double coins or a second chance, only play when you choose them. At most 12 a day.
  • No banner ads, and no ads when you open the game.
  • No breaks between levels before level 9, never right after you lose, and at most one every three wins.
  • Any purchase removes the breaks between levels for good.
  • You can turn off personalised ads any time in Settings, under Privacy & ads.
